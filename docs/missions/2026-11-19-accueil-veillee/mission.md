The owner's working names « Kompè Lapen » / « Komè Zamba » differ (Kompè/Konpè, Komè/Konpè, Zamba/Zanba). Research and document; propose the variant; raise the ambiguity to pilotage, who groups it for the owner. Do not block: use the documented variant provisionally.
- H3. Portrait drawings: original drawings made in code/SVG/painted textures by the executor, or reused project assets. No AI image generation and no third-party image without a verified compatible licence.
- H4. The tree universe (`src/tree/`) is replaced as the home, not deleted blindly: anything still used elsewhere (fallback, lite mode, Konteur, invitation) is kept or migrated with justification in `report.md`.
- H5. Mobile measurements are emulated unless the owner names a physical device (none known).
- H6. Videos: Playwright `recordVideo` (webm) from a headed GPU-backed Chrome; ffmpeg is not known to be installed (check; do not install system packages). Heavy media in `~/.local/share/contes-creole-qa/accueil-veillee/`, manifest with sha256 in `evidence/`; only light evidence committed.
